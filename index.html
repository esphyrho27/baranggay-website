A Community Built on Bayanihan

Barangay Puntod is known for its hardworking families, maritime roots, and welcoming neighborhood spirit. Over the years, the community has continued to grow while preserving its rich cultural heritage and strong sense of unity. Residents take pride in supporting one another through community initiatives, local celebrations, and shared traditions, making Barangay Puntod a vibrant and resilient place where people can live, work, and thrive together.
